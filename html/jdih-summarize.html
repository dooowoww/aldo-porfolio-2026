Informasi Hukum (JDIH)

PT INKA (Persero)

PT INKA’s JDIH is a project to develop and improve the Legal Documentation and Information Network system, which is used to manage and provide access to various regulations and legal information related to the company’s business processes.

I was involved in the development of PT INKA’s JDIH system, an internal application used to manage and provide information related to company policies and regulations relevant to business processes.

Some of the features designed include:

  • 🔐 Access ControlManage access based on document classification
  • 👥 User MonitoringEnsuring that user access aligns with employment status
  • 🔔 Review NotificationManage review needs based on document classification
  • 🏢 Stakeholder DirectoryLinking regulations to the issuing agency
  • ⚖️ External Regulation ReviewDigitizing the external regulatory review process
  • 📊 Compliance MonitoringMonitoring deadlines and reporting evidence

Feature development based on the needs of the Risk Management and Compliance Division, with a focus on improving accessibility, document security, user management, regulatory monitoring, and monitoring of reporting obligations.

Project Outcome

The development of JDIH is aimed at transforming various regulatory management and compliance processes into more structured workflows within the system, ranging from user access and document management to regulatory review and compliance reporting.
